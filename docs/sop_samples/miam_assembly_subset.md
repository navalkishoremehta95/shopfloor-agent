# MIAM assembly subset (FRAS)

Industrial assembly procedure from the MIAM / M³-HRC human–robot collaboration work.
The full MIAM assembly task has 91 steps; this subset keeps the notebook small.

- MIAM: https://github.com/navalkishoremehta95/MIAM
- M³-HRC: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ArvindSihag/M3_Multimodal_Human_Robot_Collaboration_Dataset

## STEP-01 take rpi CM
Pick up the Raspberry Pi camera module housing.

## STEP-02 take rpi camera
Depends on STEP-01. Pick up the camera sensor module.

## STEP-03 align rpi camera with CM
Depends on STEP-02. Align camera with camera module using fixture pins.

## STEP-04 take screwdriver
Depends on STEP-03. Select Phillips screwdriver from tool tray.

## STEP-05 take screw
Depends on STEP-04. Pick M2 screw for camera mount.

## STEP-06 screw screw with screwdriver
Depends on STEP-05. High risk: shared bench — keep hands clear during fastening.

## STEP-07 put rpi camera CM
Depends on STEP-06. Place assembled camera module in staging area.

## STEP-08 put screwdriver
Depends on STEP-07. Return screwdriver to tool tray.

## STEP-09 take display
Depends on STEP-08. Pick up display module.

## STEP-10 put display
Depends on STEP-09. Place display in fixture zone B.
